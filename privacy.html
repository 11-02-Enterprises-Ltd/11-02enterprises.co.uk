Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Draft for owner review before publication. The privacy contact and provider arrangements below need owner verification.

This notice explains how 11-02 Enterprises Ltd handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us. It covers this corporate website; other ventures may provide their own notices.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

11-02 Enterprises Ltd is the data controller for the personal information we use for the purposes described here. This means we decide why and how it is used.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email info@11-02enterprises.co.uk. Owner verification required before publication: confirm this existing site contact address is monitored and ready to handle privacy requests.

2. Information we receive

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional company or venture name, and your message. We also receive information you include in follow-up correspondence. Please send only what is needed for your enquiry and avoid sensitive personal information.

You choose whether to contact us. The form requires a name, email and message so we can understand and respond; the company field is optional. Without the required details, the form cannot be submitted.

Website and form requests also involve technical information such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser details and request times, processed by the providers described below.

3. Why we use information and our lawful bases

  • Handling enquiries and correspondence: our legitimate interests in answering questions, managing business relationships and considering partnership opportunities, balanced against your rights and reasonable expectations.
  • Steps towards a contract: where you ask us to take steps before entering a contract with you personally, processing necessary for those steps. Business enquiries made on behalf of a company will generally be handled under legitimate interests instead.
  • Operating and protecting the website: our legitimate interests in delivering a reliable site, preventing misuse and investigating security issues.
  • Legal obligations: where retaining or disclosing particular information is necessary to comply with an applicable legal requirement.

Submitting an enquiry does not itself give consent to unrelated marketing. Any separate use requiring consent must be explained when consent is sought.

4. Hosting and contact form providers

Vercel hosts and delivers this website. It processes connection and request data, which may include IP addresses and security or diagnostic logs, to serve pages, maintain reliability and protect the service. Processing depends on the service configuration and provider arrangements. See Vercel’s privacy notice.

Formspree is our contact form service provider. When you submit the form, your details and message are sent to Formspree for processing and delivery to us. Formspree may also process technical request information for service operation and abuse prevention. Enquiries delivered by email also involve our email service provider. See Formspree’s privacy policy.

5. Vercel Web Analytics and cookies

This website includes Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits and improve the site. Its reports can include page views, referring sites, broad location and browser or device categories. Vercel describes a cookie-free analytics design that uses a request-derived identifier rather than tracking cookies, with aggregated reporting. See Vercel’s analytics privacy documentation.

Our proposed basis for any personal data used for audience measurement is legitimate interests in understanding and improving the website, subject to an assessment of necessity, impact and applicable UK rules. Cookie-free design does not by itself establish that consent is unnecessary or that no personal data is processed. Other service or security requests may involve cookies or similar technologies.

Owner review before publication: confirm the analytics lawful basis and whether consent or a way to object is required under current UK GDPR and electronic communications rules. Any required controls must be implemented and this notice updated. This page does not provide an analytics opt-out control.

6. Google Fonts and external requests

We load DM Sans and Playfair Display through Google Fonts. Your browser connects to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, sharing your IP address and request details such as browser information and potentially a referring page. The purpose is to display the site’s typography; our proposed basis is legitimate interests in presenting a consistent, readable website, subject to owner review of the necessity and impact of external font loading. See Google’s explanation of Fonts privacy and Google’s privacy policy.

If you follow a link to an external site, that provider handles your visit under its own privacy information.

7. Sharing and international transfers

Information may be available to people within the company who need it to handle your enquiry, and to hosting, form and email providers supporting these activities. Where necessary, it may also be shared with professional advisers or authorities for legal obligations or legal claims.

Providers and their subprocessors may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. The countries and safeguards depend on the applicable provider contracts and service settings. UK transfer rules may require adequacy arrangements or appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, with any necessary assessments. The owner must verify the arrangements that actually apply before publication. Contact us for details of relevant safeguards and how to obtain a copy.

8. How long information is kept

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for its purpose. For enquiries, this depends on whether the matter is resolved, whether a business relationship follows, and any legal, accounting or claims-related need to keep records. Provider storage, logs and backups depend on their arrangements and account settings. The owner must confirm review and deletion procedures across form records, email and provider systems; no fixed retention period is specified here.

9. Your rights and complaints

Under UK data protection law, depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access your personal information, correct it, erase it, restrict its use or receive it in a portable format. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, including analytics where personal data is involved, for reasons relating to your situation. You also have an absolute right to object to direct marketing. Contact us using the email above to make a request. These rights have conditions and exceptions; we may need proportionate information to verify your identity.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator. You are welcome to raise concerns with us first, but you do not have to do so before contacting the ICO.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website or our processing changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
